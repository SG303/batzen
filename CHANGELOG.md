## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-28

First versioned release. The app already covered:

### Added

- Budget overview per month and year with costs, income, saving rate and a flow diagram
- Items and income as timelines, shared costs, due months with a reserve plan and bill calendar
- Item notes per version
- Assets with balance timelines, emergency fund and year-end totals
- Check-in with snapshots, contributions to linked assets and a reminder after six months of use
- Planning with savings goals and "what if" scenarios
- History with year comparison and a printable year review
- Login with several accounts, throttled password checks and security headers
- Nightly backups, copy before database updates, JSON export and restore
- Privacy mode, light and dark theme
- The current version at the bottom of every page
